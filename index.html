08 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

What platforms do you build on?

Mostly WordPress and Shopify, plus custom HTML/CSS/JS builds for landing pages that need to be lightweight and fast.

How long does a typical project take?

A landing page usually takes 3–7 days. Full stores or multi-page sites run 2–4 weeks depending on scope.

Do you offer post-launch support?

Yes — ongoing maintenance, updates and monitoring are available after launch, not just a one-time handoff.

Can you improve an existing site instead of rebuilding?

Often, yes. I audit first — sometimes it's a speed and SEO pass, sometimes a full rebuild makes more sense.

How do we get started?

Message me on email, phone, Telegram or Facebook with a short brief — I'll reply with next steps and a timeline.
